# Dosewell privacy policy

Effective date: 15 November 2026

Dosewell is a medicine reminder app made by Kashif Nowman Kanak, Chennai, India ("I", "me").

## Summary

Your information stays on your phone. Dosewell has no account, no server, no ads, no analytics and no trackers. I cannot see anything you enter.

## Information you enter

- People you add: name, relation, blood group, allergies, conditions, doctor's name and phone number
- Medicines: name, strength, schedule, stock count, notes
- Dose history: which doses were marked taken, skipped or missed
- Emergency contacts: name, relation, phone number
- Records: photos of prescriptions, reports or bills that you choose to add

This includes health information. All of it is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. It is not sent to me or to anyone else, and it is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.

## Sharing

Dosewell shares nothing by itself. When you tap Save on a record photo, it is saved to your phone's Downloads folder, where other apps on your phone can see it. When you tap Share, Tell a guardian, or Back up to a file, the app hands the text, picture or file to the app you choose (for example WhatsApp or Messages). What happens next is covered by that app's own policy. A backup file is not encrypted; keep it somewhere safe.

## Permissions

- Notifications, alarms, vibration and run-at-startup: to show reminders at the times you set, including after the phone restarts
- Camera: opened only when you tap Take a photo. Dosewell does not request the camera permission itself; your phone's camera app takes the picture
- Dosewell does not request the internet permission, so it cannot send or receive anything over the network

## Keeping and deleting

Information stays until you delete it. More > Erase everything removes all of it at once. Uninstalling the app also removes it. Because nothing is held on a server, there is nothing for me to delete on your behalf.

## Children

Dosewell is intended for adults, including adults who manage a child's medicines. It is not directed at children.

## Not medical advice

Dosewell is a reminder and record tool. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Always follow a qualified healthcare professional.
